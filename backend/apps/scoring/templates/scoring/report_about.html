About this assessment

How it works

  1. You sent your documents: {{ v.documents|thousands }} documents, {{ v.pages|thousands }} pages.
  2. Every page was read against {{ v.requirements }} practices in nine areas of AI readiness, four topics to an area.
  3. A practice counts when a passage in your documents shows it, and counts in part when the passage shows part of it. The detailed report names the document and page for each finding.
  4. Follow-up questions let you confirm how far each practice goes. An answer counts when a document supports it.
  5. An area's score is the share of its practices shown. The overall score combines the nine areas.

Stage 1 and final

The Stage 1 result comes from your documents alone.{% if v.held_share_text and v.held_points %} {{ v.held_share_text|capfirst }} of the credit for each well-evidenced practice is to be confirmed by your follow-up answers.{% endif %} The final result is published once the questions are closed. An answer can raise a score, and it can also lower one.

What this result can't tell you

  • Scores come only from the documents you sent. Something the {{ v.institution_word }} does but has not written down will show as missing. Send the document and it can be counted.
  • The levels are provisional until more institutions have been assessed.
  • Completing a step changes the score only when a new assessment reads the documents that show it. We suggest a new assessment every 6 to 12 months.
