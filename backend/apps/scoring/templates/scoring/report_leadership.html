How the assessment works

We read the documents your institution sent ({{ v.documents }} document{{ v.documents|pluralize }}) against {{ v.requirements }} practices in nine areas of AI readiness. Each practice earns credit when the documents show it, and part credit when they show part of it. An area's score is the share of its practices shown; the overall score combines the nine areas. Every finding in this report quotes the document it came from.

{% if v.held_points %}

The hatched part of a bar

For each practice your documents show, {{ v.held_share_text }} of its credit is held until the follow-up questions confirm how far it goes, for example how many staff, how often, with what results. {% if v.final %}Credit the answers did not confirm stays held.{% else %}Your answers, backed by documents, earn it back.{% endif %}

{% endif %}

Keep in mind

A practice we did not find in your documents may still exist; send the document and it can be counted. The levels are provisional until calibrated across colleges. Completing an action changes the score only when a new assessment reads the documents that show it.

The detailed report lists all {{ v.requirements }} practices, with what was found, the quotation and its document, and the next step for each.
