How to read this report

Each area has practices at three stages: Foundation (it is set up), In use (it happens, repeatedly) and Results (its effects are measured and reviewed). A practice is shown when your documents show all of it, partly shown when they show some of it, and not found when we found nothing yet. A practice we did not find may still exist: send the document and it can be counted.
